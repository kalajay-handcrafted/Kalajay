Our story

A single bead or a strand of yarn or a paint brush. A little patience. Something beautiful.

Kalajay began with a simple love for making things by hand. Today, we are a small business built almost entirely by women, creating pieces that bring beauty to everyday life. From jewellery boxes that hold your treasures to beaded purses, keyrings, and coasters, every piece brings together thoughtful design and patient handwork. Each is made to be as useful as it is pretty.

Our name draws from kala, meaning art. That spirit runs through everything we create. Nothing is mass-produced; each piece reflects the skill and care of its maker, with subtle differences that make it special.

From our first order to our first stall to every home we reach, we're growing one handcrafted piece at a time. Thank you for being part of our story.
