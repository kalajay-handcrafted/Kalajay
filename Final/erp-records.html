Records & action history

Admin controls for correcting records. Every saved change requires confirmation and is retained in action history.

Product deletion removes it and its varieties from the website and active inventory. Past orders, stock movements and action history are retained. To delete an associated upload, remove its image path or bill attachment from the record first, then choose Uploaded files & attachments. Files still used by another record cannot be deleted. Transaction and order corrections update stock automatically; invalid negative balances are blocked.
